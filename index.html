TASS

Text Analysis Support Software.

In development

TASS is a SIM DAD LLC tool for dictionary-based text analysis. It is in active development, and we are opening it to a small group of research partners first.

Call for beta researchers

We are inviting researchers to put TASS to work on their own corpora and help shape its first release. Participation is open to people who are currently affiliated with a university or an established research institution.

To apply, write to tass@simdadllc.com from your institutional email address. We use that address to confirm eligibility, so we are not able to consider requests sent from a personal account. A short note about your institution, your field, and how you would like to use TASS helps us match you to the beta.

Places are limited, and we review requests as they arrive.
